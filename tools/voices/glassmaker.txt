Welcome, little one. Come in, come in. This is my town, and these are my memories, kept in glass. Out past the trees the land floats, in pieces high above the clouds and far below. At the bottom of a long, long well, the heart is beating again. Your job is to go down there and talk to the people in the town first. The smith sells armor, the enchanter makes a plain blade sing, and the inn always has soup on.
