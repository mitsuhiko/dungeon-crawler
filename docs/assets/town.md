# Town service models

![All seven models](../../learn/img/model-town.jpg)

Seven texture-free, flat-shaded models using the existing `props.py` mesh builder,
vertex-colour material, neutral masonry, warm timber and muted meadow colours.
Large pitched roofs and raised pictogram signs keep services readable at game scale.

| Asset | Features | Triangles |
|---|---|---:|
| `town_merchant_a` | Ochre/cream tent, provisions, pottery, barrel, coin sign | 1,906 |
| `town_merchant_b` | Sage/cream tent, herbs, crate, leaf sign | 1,486 |
| `town_merchant_c` | Rust/cream tent, potion bottles, crate, bottle sign | 1,692 |
| `town_inn_a` | Two-storey half-timber inn, bed/healing sign, porch, bench, flower boxes | 3,588 |
| `town_stable_a` | Three stalls, two horses, hay, hitching rail, trough, horse sign | 2,730 |
| `town_pet_shop_a` | Paw sign, striped awning, rabbit pen, cat, kennels, bird perch, side window | 3,832 |
| `town_blacksmith_a` | Open forge, emissive coals, chimney, anvil/hammer, bellows, quench tub, side window | 2,766 |

## Files

- `blender/town.py`: reproducible procedural source; depends on sibling `props.py`.
- `blender/town.blend`: editable showroom with all seven assets, camera and lighting.
- `assets/town.glb`: combined asset library; each named root is at the origin. With
  `town_trees.glb` it is the `town` prop set (loaded when a cube shows it).
- `assets/town/<asset>.glb`: standalone model; `blender/previews/town/<asset>.png`: individual preview.
- `blender/previews/town-preview.png`: overview render (previews are local, not committed; the
  picture above is a copy in `learn/img/`, `node tools/preview-images.mjs`).
- `assets/town/manifest.json`: triangle counts, dimensions, authoring bounds and service hints.

## Game contract

- **1 unit = 1 game cell.** Use scale 1; the game's character is approximately 1.1 units tall.
- Blender: Z up, front -Y. Export: **Y up, front +Z**. Ground is Y=0 in glTF.
- Origins are at the main building's ground-centred footprint, not the centre of attached props.
  Bounds include barrels, porches, fences, kennels and workshop attachments.
- Each Blender asset is one mesh with flat normals and baked geometry; no textures, UVs,
  modifiers, rigs or animation. Each GLB root has two material primitives: vertex colour
  and emissive lantern/forge; the houses with windows (inn, pet shop, blacksmith) a third,
  `<name>_window`: the windows' outer panes, which the game lights at night only, a light out
  of each (`render/props.js`, `render/night.js`; by day they are plain glass). A loader may
  therefore expose a group with two or three mesh children.
- Extras: `service`, `stock`, `variant`, `attach`, `blocking`, `radius`, `footprint`,
  `height`, `light`, `intensity`, `units`, `front`, `cube_crawler_town`.
- `light`/`intensity` are optional runtime lighting hints, not exported light objects.
- Showroom positions, labels, floor, camera and lights are excluded from every GLB.

These are **exterior visual assets only**. The game places the four permanent
buildings in an asymmetric starting town and the three merchant tents in the
next cube along its outgoing road, all on the grid (quarter turns). Both clearings
reserve straight walking paths. The inn, blacksmith and pet shop have colliders that
follow their walls and props (`parts` in `src/world/town.js`) and a door that leads to
an empty interior in the house's proportions (`docs/world.md`, House interiors); the stable
and tents collide with their whole footprint. Sleeping/healing, trading, pet ownership and horse interactions are not
implemented. Animals are static decoration joined into the buildings.
Buildings are wider than the one-cell scatter props; they are placed explicitly,
not randomly through the decoration generator. `radius` is a spacing hint, not a collider.

The surrounding grove uses three larger trees from `assets/town_trees.glb`, generated
by `blender/town_trees.py` (editable source `blender/town_trees.blend`, preview
`blender/previews/town_trees-preview.png`). These are 6–7 units tall, with broad faceted crowns.
Their `trunk_radius` / `trunk_height` metadata distinguishes collision from foliage;
canopies overhang paths and are handled by the player's cutaway. The town plan
and safety rules live in `src/world/town.js`; see `docs/world.md`.

## Town clutter

The clearings' own small things come from `assets/town_clutter.glb` (also the `town` prop
set), generated by `blender/town_clutter.py` (editable source `blender/town_clutter.blend`,
preview `blender/previews/town_clutter-preview.png`), in the same palette: street lamps (a
lantern on a post, a taller post with two hanging lanterns, and a wooden crook with one hanging
from it: their glass is the `_glow` material, lit at night only, like the windows), two benches,
a well with its roof and bucket, two hand carts (sacks, or hay and a crate), flower pots, hay
bales, a woodpile with a chopping block, a notice board, two hedges and two dry stone walls (two
cells long), and the garlands: two poles and three segments of a string of bulbs and pennants
(`town_garland_a`–`c`, attach `line`: the cord from the origin along X to 1, as the dark forest's
lantern strings; `src/world/town.js` shears each along the string's sag, `matrix`). Each is one
mesh, its origin at the middle of its foot (a garland's at its cord's start), front -Y in
Blender; `blocking` ones get a collider where `src/world/town.js` puts them.

## The wall

The wall round the town comes from `assets/town_walls.glb` (also the `town` prop set), generated
by `blender/town_walls.py` (editable source `blender/town_walls.blend`, preview
`blender/previews/town_walls-preview.png`): a stretch of crenellated wall a cell long in three
kinds (plain, ivy over it, a lantern on its inner face), an arch for where it crosses the water
(a grate in it; one with a lantern), a round tower open on top with a brazier or under a pointed
roof with a banner, and the gatehouse (two square towers, the arch over the way between them, its
gates open, the portcullis up, lanterns either side, banners with the glass bird). Each stands on
a footing under the ground's line (`foot`: 0.9, an arch's 1.1 under the water's), its outer face
-Y in Blender (+Z in glTF, to the forest), and carries `cube_crawler_town_wall`; the lanterns and
braziers are the `_glow` material, lit at night only.

## Homes and yards

The people's homes and what stands round them come from `assets/town_homes.glb` (also the `town`
prop set), generated by `blender/town_homes.py` (editable source `blender/town_homes.blend`,
preview `blender/previews/town_homes-preview.png`), in the same palette: four cottages
(`town_cottage_a`–`d`), a barn and a shed, two fences, two garden beds, two laundry lines, two
market stalls, a trough, beehives, a chicken coop, two planters and a scarecrow. Each carries
`cube_crawler_town_home`; the houses face their door (front +Z in glTF, where their path leads),
have no inside (their doors stay shut) and collide with their footprint. `src/world/town.js` (`HOMES`, `plotTown`) lays them out along the town's ways.

The same file has the mill by the river (`town_watermill_a`: a stone storey, a timber one, its
windows lit at night, the wheel's axle out of its +X side `AXLE` (0.45) over its floor) and its
wheel (`town_waterwheel_a`: two rims on spokes, paddles between them, its foot at the origin and
its hub `WHEEL_R` (1.05) over it, its axle along X). The wheel carries `spin` (how high its hub is)
and `spin_rate` (radians a second): the game turns it about its hub (`src/render/props.js`), each
in its own time. `src/world/townland.js` finds a place for the mill on a bank where the river runs
straight, `src/world/town.js` (`watermill`) puts the wheel in the water beside it, its hub on the
axle.

## Rebuild / validate

From the repository root (replace `Blender` with your executable):

```sh
Blender --background --python blender/town.py
Blender --background --python blender/town_clutter.py
Blender --background --python blender/town_homes.py
Blender --background --python blender/town_walls.py
Blender --background --python blender/tests/test_town.py
npm run compress-assets                        # then compress (CONTRIBUTING.md)
```

The build exports before laying out the showroom. Tests check all seven meshes,
deterministic rebuilds, triangle budgets, flat shading, ground pivots and the actual
combined/individual GLBs: Y-up bounds, named roots, vertex colours, emissive materials,
service extras, no unwanted preview objects, and restoration of preview positions.
